How we handle your personal data.

What data the website collects

  • Online booking: full name, email, phone (optional), date, time and format (online) of the appointment, and the language of the meeting.
  • Request to be contacted: name, email and/or phone, preferred times and an optional short message.
  • Technical data: to protect the system from abuse, a non-reversible cryptographic fingerprint of the IP address, linked to the number of requests, is kept for a few days.

The website doesn't ask you to describe why you're booking or for any health information. Please don't include it in the optional fields. The mere fact of booking with a psychology practice could still be considered health-related data: for this reason the data is handled with enhanced security measures.

Why the data is processed, and on what basis

  • To arrange and manage the appointment you requested (confirmation, reminder, changes and cancellations): pre-contractual and contractual measures taken at your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); for any health-related data, healthcare provided by a professional bound by professional secrecy (Art. 9(2)(h) and 9(3) GDPR).
  • To answer a request to be contacted: pre-contractual measures at your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • System security and abuse prevention: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

The data is not used for marketing, profiling or advertising, and is not sent to analytics tools or social networks.

How it is protected

Name, email, phone and messages are encrypted before being stored in the database. The agenda can be accessed only by the practice's psychologists and by whoever administers it, each with her own account, a password and a second authentication factor; each psychologist sees only her own appointments. Confirmation and reminder emails contain no clinical information.

How long it is kept

  • Booking data: for the duration of the relationship and up to [24] months after the last appointment, then contact details are deleted automatically.
  • Requests to be contacted: up to [6] months.
  • Anti-abuse technical fingerprints: 7 days.

This is without prejudice to the retention obligations under tax and professional rules for the records of the services provided, which are managed separately from this website.

Your rights

You can ask at any time for access, rectification, erasure, restriction or portability of your data, and object to processing based on legitimate interest, by writing to {{contact.email}}. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (www.garanteprivacy.it).

Whether you have to provide the data

Name and email are needed to confirm an online booking; you can always phone instead. The other fields are optional.
