What data the website collects

  • Online request for a first meeting: full name, email, phone (optional), the area or service chosen (for example career guidance, coaching, psychological support or psychotherapy), the psychologist, the language of the meeting, the date, time and format (online) of the appointment, whether you confirmed you're coming and which of our emails you confirmed from, whether the psychologist recorded that you didn't attend and, if you choose to answer, how you heard about us (see "Statistics, without tracking").
  • Request to be contacted: name, email and/or phone, area, preferred psychologist, language of the meeting, preferred times and an optional short message.
  • Requests from organisations: also the organisation's name, the sender's role and the size of the organisation.
  • Notice version: with each booking or request we record which version of this notice you read.
  • Technical data: to protect the system from abuse, a non-reversible cryptographic fingerprint of the IP address, linked to the number of requests, is kept for a few days.

The website doesn't ask you to describe why you're booking or for any health information. Please don't include it in the optional fields. The mere fact of booking with a psychology practice could still be considered health-related data: for this reason the data is handled with enhanced security measures.

Why the data is processed, and on what basis

  • To arrange and manage the appointment you requested (acceptance or decline, request to confirm you're coming, reminders, changes and cancellations): pre-contractual and contractual measures taken at your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); for any health-related data, healthcare provided by a professional bound by professional secrecy (Art. 9(2)(h) and 9(3) GDPR).
  • To answer a request to be contacted: pre-contractual measures at your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • System security and abuse prevention: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Internal statistics (see below): legitimate interest in knowing whether the website and the service work (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). [to be checked with your adviser]

The data is not used for marketing, profiling or advertising, and is not sent to social networks or to other companies' analytics tools.

Statistics, without tracking

To see whether the website and the service work, we count a few things. What we look at are totals; the few details kept with an appointment are described below, with how long they stay.

  • Website: how often each page is viewed, what kind of site people arrive from (for example a search engine or a social network, never the full address), whether they're on a phone or a computer, which buttons are clicked, and how far people get with a form or a booking. Only when a booking or request goes through do we also count which page and which button led to it. The counting sets no cookies, stores nothing on your device and keeps no IP address or other identifier, so we can't tell how many different people visit or follow anyone from page to page. No analytics company is involved: our server adds one to counters for each day. If your browser sends a "Global Privacy Control" or "Do Not Track" signal, nothing is counted. More details are on the cookies page. [what Netlify records in its own technical logs: to check]
  • Emails: for each type of email (for example "reminder"), each language and each day, we count how many were sent and, from what our email provider reports, how many were delivered, bounced, marked as spam or blocked. No email addresses are kept in these counts. Our system doesn't measure whether you open an email or click its links [whether Brevo measures this on its own depends on the account settings: to check]. The links for managing your appointment carry only the type of email they came from; it is counted only if you then click to confirm, cancel or move the appointment, so we can see which emails lead to which actions.
  • "How did you hear about us?": answering is optional, and you choose from a fixed list. On the contact and organisations forms, your answer is only added to that month's total and isn't saved with your request. When you book a first meeting, the answer is kept with the appointment for a few months, so we can count, for each kind of source, how many people came to their first meeting and how many had a session within 30, 60 and 90 days. Ninety days after the end of the month of your first meeting, it is deleted and only the monthly totals remain. If you move the meeting, the answer moves with it and is counted once; the 90 days then run from the month of the new date. If you ask us to delete your data, this answer goes too. [to be checked with your adviser]
  • Appointments: the practice works out overall figures from its agenda, such as how many requests come in, how quickly they're answered, how many appointments are cancelled, and how many people carry on after a first meeting. For this, the appointment record also keeps which of our emails you confirmed from and, if it happens, the fact that you didn't attend: the psychologist can record this only after the start time of a confirmed appointment, and can undo it. Each psychologist sees only her own figures, and whoever administers the practice sees the figures for the whole practice and for each psychologist, as well as the website and email statistics. Whenever someone looks at figures that aren't their own (the whole practice's or a colleague's), and in downloaded files, breakdowns of 1 to 4 are shown as "fewer than 5", and in the more detailed tables other figures are hidden too, so small numbers can't be worked out by subtraction. Downloaded files contain no names or contact details.

Daily counters are kept for about 25 months. After that, the website counters are added up into monthly totals and the email counters are deleted. The monthly totals are kept with no set end date. None of these counts contains names, contact details or IP addresses. [retention period and whether the totals count as personal data: to be checked with your adviser]

How an online request works

When you request a free first meeting, the time is held for you and the psychologist sees your request in her agenda. The email that tells her about a new request contains no personal data and goes only to her. If you chose "Help me choose", the request is assigned automatically to a psychologist who is free then, and she may pass it to her colleague. She accepts or declines it, and we email you the outcome. If she hasn't accepted it by 6 pm (Italian time) the day before, the request expires, the time is released and we email you. About two days before an appointment, we email you to ask you to confirm you're coming, with a link to a page on this website; if you haven't confirmed, we email you once more the day before, and the evening before, the psychologist sees which appointments are still unconfirmed, so she can write to you or free the time. If she cancels or frees the time, we email you. Two hours before, we send a short reminder. For appointments booked by your psychologist, these emails are sent only if you've agreed to receive them. None of these emails contains clinical information, and the subject line names only Blooming, never the psychologist or the kind of help.

How it is protected

Name, email, phone and messages are encrypted before being stored in the database. The agenda can be accessed only by the practice's psychologists and by whoever administers it, each with her own account, a password and a second authentication factor. Each psychologist sees only her own appointments and the requests addressed to her; whoever administers the practice sees all agendas and the requests with no preferred psychologist [who holds this role: to confirm]. Searches, exports and deletions of personal data are recorded in a log. Appointment emails contain no clinical information.

How long it is kept

  • Booking data: for the duration of the relationship and up to [24] months after the last appointment, then name, email, phone and messages are deleted automatically. Only the appointment stays in the agenda, with nothing that identifies you.
  • Requests to be contacted: up to [6] months.
  • Anti-abuse technical fingerprints: 7 days.
  • Your answer to "How did you hear about us?" on a booking: until 90 days after the end of the month of your first meeting, or sooner if you ask us to delete your data.
  • Statistics counters: website counters for about 25 months day by day, then as monthly totals; email counters for about 25 months, then deleted; monthly totals with no set end date [to be checked with your adviser].

This is without prejudice to the retention obligations under tax and professional rules for the records of the services provided, which are managed separately from this website.

Your rights

You can ask at any time for access, rectification, erasure, restriction or portability of your data, and object to processing based on legitimate interest, by writing to {{contact.email}}. We can find, send you or delete your data from the email address you used. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (www.garanteprivacy.it).

Whether you have to provide the data

Name and email are needed to send an online request; you can always phone instead. The other fields are optional.
