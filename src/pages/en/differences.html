The differences

Psychologist, psychotherapist or coach: the differences, and who does what at Blooming.

Professional titles don't always mean the same thing from one country to another, so what you called a therapist or a counsellor at home may correspond to a different title here. This page explains what each title means in Italy, who does what at Blooming, and how to choose.

01 · The professions

Five titles you may come across, and what they mean in Italy.

  • Psychologist. A psychologist has a degree in psychology and is registered with the Order of Psychologists of their region, which keeps a public register you can check. On this site we call it the Italian Order of Psychologists. Psychologists can offer psychological assessment and support, and many also work in career guidance.
  • Scope: to verify with a source, e.g. Law 56/1989.
  • Psychotherapist. A psychotherapist is a psychologist or a medical doctor who has completed at least four years of specialist training in psychotherapy. Psychotherapy is for difficulties that go deeper or have lasted a long time.
  • Four years: to verify with a source. Usually longer than psychological support: to confirm with Monica.
  • Psychiatrist. A psychiatrist is a medical doctor who specialises in mental health and can prescribe medication. Blooming doesn't offer psychiatry.
  • Coach. Coaching isn't a regulated profession in Italy, so coaches come from many different backgrounds and kinds of training. Coaching works on goals and next steps, usually at work, and it isn't therapy.
  • Not regulated: to check with your own adviser.
  • Counsellor. In the UK and many other countries, "counselling" is a common word for talking therapy. In Italy the word is used differently. If you're looking for what you'd have called counselling at home, the closest service at Blooming is psychological support.
  • Wording to check with your own adviser.

03 · How to choose

Start from what's going on, not from the job title.

  • You and your new manager keep clashing, and every meeting leaves you tense. Workplace coaching with Elsa.

  • You're weighing up whether to take a job in another city, retrain or stay where you are. Career coaching & guidance with Elsa.

  • Your mother, in another country, has just been diagnosed with a serious illness, and you can't concentrate on anything else. Psychological support with Monica.

  • You think you may need medication. A psychiatrist, or your GP to start with. Blooming doesn't offer psychiatry.

It's normal for the line not to be clear. When it isn't, the free first meeting is there to sort it out.

You don't need to choose before you book.

In a free 30-minute video call, you describe what's going on and find out which kind of help fits, and with whom. It isn't a session or an assessment.

You can book with Elsa or Monica directly, or pick "Help me choose": you'll meet one of the psychologists who works in the language you chose, and together you work out what fits. If it turns out to be the other psychologist's area, she can suggest her colleague.
