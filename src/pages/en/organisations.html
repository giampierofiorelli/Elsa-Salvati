For organisations

A healthier workplace culture is possible.

Good people leave, and the reason given is always "personal". A promising hire is gone within six months. A team gets the work done, but nobody says what they really think. When this keeps happening, the cause is rarely one person.

01 · Where we start

Culture is made of everyday decisions.

Who gets hired and how, how roles and responsibilities are split, how a team works together, what training is actually needed. Elsa, a psychologist, has worked in HR for over ten years, in recruitment, training and change management, with international experience. Dagmara works on organisational process analysis.

Dagmara's experience: to confirm.

We look at those decisions with you, in English or Italian, and help you change the ones that matter most. We'd rather fix a few specific things properly than promise to transform your culture.

02 · What we work on

Five areas, from processes to people.

From the client's brief; each item stays only if confirmed in the questionnaire.

  1. 01

    Processes and roles

    We map how your HR processes work today, through interviews and surveys with the people involved, and redesign workflows, roles and structure where needed.

  2. 02

    Hiring for key roles

    We help you define the profile, assess skills and follow up once the person starts.

  3. 03

    Team coaching

    For teams that need to work together better, handle a conflict, get through a change or find their motivation again.

  4. 04

    Tailored training

    We work out what's needed and design the programme, with you if you like. We deliver it, or refer you to a specialist training provider.

  5. 05

    Outplacement

    For people who have to leave.

    Regulatory aspects to check with your own adviser.

We can also offer your employees coaching, psychological support and psychotherapy. They help the person; what isn't working in the organisation needs dealing with separately, and we'll tell you so. The organisation receives no information about what is said in sessions.
