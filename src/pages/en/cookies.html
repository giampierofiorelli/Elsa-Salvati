Cookies

Cookies and tracking tools.

This website uses no profiling, advertising or statistics cookies, and loads no third-party content (fonts, maps, videos, social media). It counts visits anonymously, without cookies and without recognising you. That's why you don't see a cookie banner.

Technical tools

The website uses only technical tools, which don't identify or follow you:

  • Chosen language (cookie nf_lang, lasts one year): set only if you switch language on the website, to show you pages in the language you chose.
  • Opening animation (your browser's local storage, bl-loader): remembers that you've already seen the opening animation. It isn't sent to anyone.
  • Reserved area (session cookie, 8 hours): only for the psychologists who sign in to the agenda. It is never set for people who visit the website or book an appointment.

List to be checked by a privacy consultant before publication.

Anonymous statistics, without cookies

To learn which pages and buttons are useful, each page you view sends our server a small message with: the page, the kind of site you came from (a search engine, for example, never the full address), the previous page on this website, the buttons you clicked, how far you got with a form, and whether you're on a phone or a computer. The server adds one to daily counters and keeps nothing else.

  • no cookies, and nothing saved on your device;
  • no IP address, no identifier, no counting of people;
  • no outside company: the figures stay with the practice;
  • if your browser sends "Global Privacy Control" or "Do Not Track", no message is sent;
  • daily counters are kept for 25 months, then reduced to monthly totals.

On the practice's own devices, staff can choose not to be counted: the choice stays in the browser's local storage (blooming-no-stats) and is never sent.

Under the Italian Data Protection Authority's guidelines of 10 June 2021, tools of this kind don't need a cookie banner: to be checked with your privacy consultant.

External links

Links to WhatsApp, phone or email open external services only if you use them: from that point, those services' own notices apply.

If anything changes

If analytics or other non-technical cookies are ever introduced, this page will be updated and, where required, you'll be asked for consent before they are switched on, with a "Reject" button as simple as "Accept".
