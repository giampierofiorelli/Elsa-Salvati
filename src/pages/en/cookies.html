Cookies

Cookies and tracking tools.

This website uses no profiling, advertising or statistics cookies, and loads no third-party content (fonts, maps, videos, social media). That's why you don't see a cookie banner.

Technical tools

The website uses only technical tools, which don't identify or follow you:

  • Chosen language (cookie nf_lang, lasts one year): set only if you switch language on the website, to show you pages in the language you chose.
  • Opening animation (your browser's local storage, bl-loader): remembers that you've already seen the opening animation. It isn't sent to anyone.
  • Reserved area (session cookie, 8 hours): only for the psychologists who sign in to the agenda. It is never set for people who visit the website or book an appointment.

List to be checked by a privacy consultant before publication.

External links

Links to WhatsApp, phone or email open external services only if you use them: from that point, those services' own notices apply.

If anything changes

If analytics or other non-technical cookies are ever introduced, this page will be updated and, where required, you'll be asked for consent before they are switched on, with a "Reject" button as simple as "Accept".
