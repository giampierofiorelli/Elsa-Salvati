Cookies

Cookies and tracking tools.

This website uses no profiling, advertising or statistics cookies, and loads no third-party content (fonts, maps, videos, social media). It counts visits as totals, without cookies and without recognising you. That's why you don't see a cookie banner. [to be checked with your adviser]

Technical tools

The website uses only technical tools, which don't identify or follow you:

  • Chosen language (cookie nf_lang, lasts one year): set only when you pick a language yourself, either with the language switch or by closing the note that offers the other version of the site. It means you get pages in that language next time.
  • Staff sign-in (cookie __Host-agenda, lasts 8 hours): only for practice staff who sign in to the admin area. In that area the browser's local storage also remembers whether the side menu is collapsed. None of this is ever set for people who visit the website or book a meeting.

The public pages save nothing in your browser's storage (local storage or session storage). The only thing they read from it is a setting that staff can switch on from the admin area, explained below. [Only on staging and on a local computer, a trial panel for colours and labels saves its choice in local storage (bl-palette2, bl-desc); it never appears on the live site. Until 7 November 2026 the opening animation saved bl-loader in local storage: the website no longer writes or reads it, so it may linger only in browsers that visited earlier versions.]

Visit statistics, without cookies

To learn which pages and buttons are useful, each page you view sends our server a short message when you leave it or switch to another tab. It says which page it was, what kind of site you came from (for example a search engine, never the full address), whether you're on a phone or a computer, which buttons you clicked, and how far you got with a form or a booking, never what you typed. Only when a booking or request goes through does the message also include the previous page on this website and which button led to it. When you click a link to the booking page, the button's name is added to the address for a moment (?da=…) and removed as soon as the page opens. Pages for managing an appointment are counted under one fixed name, never with their address. The server adds one to counters for each day and keeps nothing else.

  • no cookies set and nothing saved on your device for this;
  • no IP address, no identifier, no counting of people;
  • no analytics company: the figures go only to the practice's own system;
  • if your browser sends "Global Privacy Control" or "Do Not Track", no message is sent;
  • daily counters are kept for about 25 months, then added up into monthly totals, which are kept for 5 years [to be checked with your adviser].

If you've switched language, your browser sends the language cookie with this message, as it does with every request to this website. The server doesn't read it.

People who work at the practice can stop their own visits being counted, from the admin area. The choice is saved in that browser's local storage (blooming-no-stats) and is never sent.

Statistics on emails and appointments are described in the privacy notice.

External links

Links to WhatsApp, phone or email open external services only if you use them: from that point, those services' own notices apply.

If anything changes

If third-party analytics or non-technical cookies are ever introduced, this page will be updated and, where required, you'll be asked for consent before they are switched on, with a "Reject" button as simple as "Accept".
