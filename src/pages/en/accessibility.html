The standard we follow

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. Published by the W3C, they're the most widely used international standard for accessible websites.

Where we are: partially conformant

This website is partially conformant with WCAG 2.2 level AA. Our checks of the public pages found no serious issues, but some tests haven't been done yet and some content is outside our control. The details are below.

Known limitations

  • Screen reader testing. We haven't yet tested the site with the main screen readers or with disabled people. [to do: timing to be decided]
  • Video calls. Online sessions run on a video-call service provided by another company, so how accessible it is depends on that provider. [service still to be chosen] If it's difficult for you, email us before your appointment and we'll find another way together. [e.g. by phone: to be confirmed with the practice]
  • PDF documents. Any PDFs we publish later may be less accessible than the web pages. If you can't read one, email us and we'll send you the content in another format. [to be confirmed with the practice]
  • Content added later. The practice updates its own text and photos. The editor asks for a description of every image and keeps headings in order, but new content doesn't go through the same automated checks.
  • Other services. Links to WhatsApp, your phone or email open apps run by other companies, which have their own accessibility.
  • Staff area. The admin area used by the practice's staff isn't covered by this statement.

How we checked

We assessed the site ourselves on 8 November 2026, across all 32 public pages in Italian and English:

  • automated testing with axe at desktop and phone widths, with no serious or critical issues;
  • colour contrast measured on the actual pixels wherever text sits on a gradient or a photo;
  • keyboard-only use, with the focus always visible;
  • 200% zoom and narrow screens down to 320 pixels, with no sideways scrolling.

We fixed the problems we found. Animations stop if you've turned on "reduce motion" on your device.
