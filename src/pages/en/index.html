02 · The differences

Coaching, psychological support and psychotherapy do different jobs.

  • Career and workplace coaching

    For making decisions about work and acting on them.

  • Psychological support

    For getting through a hard time, sometimes in just a few sessions.

  • Psychotherapy

    When the difficulty runs deep or has lasted a long time.

A problem at work doesn't automatically call for therapy. In the first meeting we work out together what kind of help fits.

03 · The first meeting

The first meeting is free, takes 30 minutes, and there's no obligation to continue.

Finding a professional who really works in English can feel like looking for a needle in a haystack. In 30 free minutes you can find out whether we're the right fit.

  1. 01

    Pick a time and, if you want, the person you'd like to see. Not sure? Pick "Help me choose": in the first meeting we work out together which of us is right for you.

  2. 02

    On a video call you tell us what's going on, and you find out whether we can help and how we'd work. It isn't a session, and nobody will push you to continue.

  3. 03

    If you'd like to go on, you plan the next steps with her. If it doesn't feel right, you can switch to someone else or stop there. No follow-up emails.

06 · The name

Why "Blooming".

To bloom is to come through a hard time, find your own resources again and feel well again, in your own time. That's why our logo is a lotus with the Greek letter Ψ, the symbol of psychology, inside it. It's what we want to make possible for the people who come to us.
