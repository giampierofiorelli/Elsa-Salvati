Fees & booking

Booking starts with a free 30-minute first meeting, online.

You book a time on the website and meet one of the psychologists by video. After that, you decide whether to go on. This page explains each step and how sessions work.

01 · How booking works

Four steps, from booking a time to deciding how to go on.

You don't need to know what kind of help you need before you book. Working that out is part of the first meeting.

  1. 01

    Book online

    Say what you'd like help with, or choose "Not sure yet". Then choose Elsa or Monica, or "Help me choose", and the language of the meeting, English or Italian. Pick a time and leave your name and email; a phone number is optional. A confirmation email arrives straight away, with a link to move or cancel the meeting.

    Monica in English: to confirm. If she doesn't confirm English: "In English, the first meeting is with Elsa, who can suggest Monica (sessions in Italian) if that's what you need." (Technical: "Help me choose" assigns, at confirmation, the psychologist with the fewest upcoming appointments among those who work in the meeting's language.)

  2. 02

    Have the free first meeting

    It lasts 30 minutes, by video call. It isn't a session or a diagnostic assessment. You say what's going on, and you find out whether the practice can help and how. It doesn't commit you to anything.

  3. 03

    Agree a plan, if you want to go on

    You and your psychologist agree what to work on, and she arranges the following sessions with you directly, not through the website.

  4. 04

    Change course, or stop

    If what you need turns out to fit the other psychologist's work, you can move to her. You can stop at any point.

    Monica in English: to confirm. "No follow-up emails": to confirm with the practice (appointment reminders aside).

03 · Practical details

Sessions are online, by video call.

  • Where. Online, by video call, from anywhere in Italy, or before you arrive.
  • Moving or cancelling. You can move or cancel the free first meeting from the link in your confirmation email. To move or cancel a later session, contact your psychologist.
  • Session length (50/60 minutes), video platform, what you need (a quiet room, a device with camera and microphone, what happens if the connection drops, whether sessions are recorded, informed consent before you start), notice for moving or cancelling, payment and invoices: to confirm. 04 · FAQ

Common questions before you book.

Can I start before I move?

Yes. Sessions are online, so you can begin while you're still abroad, for example to plan your job search before you arrive.

The rules for online sessions, especially psychological ones, may depend on the country you're in: to check with your own adviser.

Do I need to speak Italian?

No. Elsa works in English.

If Monica confirms English: "Monica offers psychological support and psychotherapy in English too." If not: "Psychological support and psychotherapy with Monica are in Italian."

What if I don't know what kind of help I need?

That's fine. You might only know that something isn't right, at work, at home or both. When you book, choose "Not sure yet"; the first meeting is there to work out what would fit, if anything.

Do I need to explain my problem when I book?

No. Your name and email are enough. You'll be asked what kind of help you're looking for, and "Not sure yet" is always an option.

Can I book just one session?

To confirm with Elsa and Monica, for example a single CV review or one mock interview.

How often do we meet, and for how long?

You agree this with your psychologist after the first meeting, and you can review it as you go.

Usual frequency and number of sessions by area: to confirm.

Can I change psychologist?

Yes, if what you need fits the other psychologist's work: Elsa works on careers and work, Monica on psychological support and psychotherapy. If it doesn't feel right, tell your psychologist, or contact the practice. You can stop at any point.

Monica in English: to confirm.

Is what I say confidential?

Elsa and Monica are bound by professional confidentiality, and they only talk to each other about you with your consent. On this site, your name, email and phone number are stored encrypted, and the site uses no tracking tools.

Confidentiality wording to check with your own adviser; how they share information: to define; video platform and its security: to confirm.

If my employer pays, what does it find out?

To define with the practice: for example, nothing about what is said in sessions; whether the service was used, only with your consent. Check with your own adviser.

Can I claim the cost on my health insurance?

What invoices the practice issues, and whether international insurers accept them: to confirm.

Can I use WhatsApp?

WhatsApp is for arranging appointments. Anything about how you're feeling is best kept for your sessions.

WhatsApp use: to confirm.

Can I get medication?

No. A psychiatrist is a medical doctor and can prescribe medication; Blooming doesn't offer psychiatry.

What if it's an emergency?

Blooming isn't an emergency service. If you or someone else is in danger, call 112 or 118, or go to the nearest emergency department (Pronto Soccorso).
