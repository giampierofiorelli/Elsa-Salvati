Team

Elsa Salvati and Monica Federico, two psychologists who work together.

Blooming is a small psychology and coaching practice. Elsa and Monica work online, by video call, with people living in Italy or about to move here. A job search that drags on can start to affect your sleep, and a hard time at home can make work feel impossible. That's why one practice offers both career help and psychological help. The name comes from the same idea: to bloom is to come through a difficult time, find your own resources and reach a point where you feel well again.

03 · Working with both

Elsa and Monica can work alongside each other when work and life get tangled up.

Say a parent back home becomes seriously ill, and you start seeing Monica to get through the months of calls and flights. Once things are steadier, the job you put on hold comes back into view, and Monica can suggest a few sessions with Elsa to plan your next step. You decide whether to go ahead. With your consent, Elsa and Monica can talk to each other.
